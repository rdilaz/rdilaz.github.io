THE IDEA

One blank canvas. Every model.

Play anything. Pick an AI. Watch what it thinks music looks like.

The model never hears your song. It creates an instrument that can see music through the same standardized audio signals every other model receives.

Your audio stays on this device. Generated visualizers run inside an isolated sandbox with no access to your model key, account data, storage, or the network.

For nerds

Prompt: visualizer-prompt-v1. Runtime: visualizer-audio-v1. The host provides normalized loudness, six frequency bands, waveform, spectrum, transient/beat information, spectral motion, rough tempo, stereo features, pointer state, and viewport information.
