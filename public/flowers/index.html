About Flowers

Each night what you closed on your Tasks list blooms here. A flower for each thing finished, a bee for each thing still open, a moth on a bowed stem for each thing you said mattered and skipped, and a butterfly for tomorrow's one thing. The arrows up top go back to earlier nights.

Music: Chopin's Polonaise in A-flat, Op. 53 ("Heroic", public domain), abridged for this page and written out by ear (not note-for-note from the score), played on the Salamander Grand Piano samples by Alexander Holm, CC BY 3.0. Type: Bodoni Moda (Owen Earl), Figtree (Erik Kennedy) and IBM Plex Mono (IBM), all from Google Fonts. Garden drawn with three.js. Flowers is the evening half of Ṛta; Sunrise is the morning.
